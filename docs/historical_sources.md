# Historical provenance (accessed 2026-09-16)

Verified museum records, used only for listed fields:

- [The Tank Museum, Panther](https://tankmuseum.org/tank-nuts/tank-collection/panther/): 44 t, 55 km/h, KwK 42 L/70. The example was completed under British supervision after surrender; not an unqualified late-1944 configuration reference.
- [The Tank Museum, Tiger I](https://tankmuseum.org/tank-nuts/tank-collection/tiger-i/): 57 t, 40 km/h, 88 mm family. Tiger 131 is NOT the target late-production visual configuration.
- [The Tank Museum, Jagdpanther](https://tankmuseum.org/tank-nuts/tank-collection/jagdpanther/): 45.5 t, 46 km/h, PaK 43 L/71; fixed superstructure and limited traverse. Its museum example also has postwar completion history.
- [The Tank Museum, T-34/85](https://tankmuseum.org/tank-nuts/tank-collection/t-34-85/): 32 t, 55 km/h, 85 mm. The collection includes Factory 112 and postwar examples; this does not prove Factory 183 geometry.
- [David Byrden, turret dimensions](https://tiger1.info/EN/Turret-dimensions.html): distinguishes factory drawing dimensions from survey estimates. Candidate for a corrected Tiger mesh; source photograph/drawing not redistributed.

Every JSON parameter not explicitly sourced above is marked approximation.
The numeric AP tables are provisional illustrative envelopes, not extracted
from War Thunder or an archival proof test. They require replacement with
traceable, test-condition-aware curves before historical analysis.
