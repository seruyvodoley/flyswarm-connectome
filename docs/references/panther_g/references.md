## Running-gear reference conflict

The requested “nine major wheel positions” conflicts with the eight
load-carrying axles recorded in the US War Department's 16 December 1943
*Tactical and Technical Trends*, no. 40:
https://ww2.lonesentry.com/articles/ttt/panther-panzer-v.html
(accessed 2026-09-16). This is primary wartime intelligence, with errors
elsewhere (including gun designation), and predates the selected G variant.
Do not convert the blockout to nine road-wheel stations solely from the brief.
Final late-G station geometry still requires variant-specific drawing/photo
alignment. Drive sprocket/idler are not road-wheel stations.
