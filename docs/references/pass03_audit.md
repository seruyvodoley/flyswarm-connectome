# Pass 03 reference and silhouette audit — 2026-09-17

Before rebuilding the five rejected assets, inspected actual museum photographs:

- Panther: Musée des Blindés own front-left high-angle photograph,
  https://museedesblindes.fr/les_chars/panther/ — glacis, turret front/side,
  wheel interleaving, fender/skirts and equipment. Museum A-type front visor /
  Zimmerit details are NOT copied into the selected G variant.
- Jagdpanther G1 at Saumur: photographer's image at
  https://historeich.blogspot.com/2017/03/sdkfz-273-jagdpanzer-v-jagdpanther-n232.html
  — single continuous glacis, recessed gun mount, tapered saukopf, roof height,
  chassis relationship. No generic box stacked on a Panther upper hull.
- T-34-85, Bovington 412: photographer's original
  https://www.flickr.com/photos/brighton/5977807273 — turret cheek shape,
  lowered nose, driver's hatch and MG, dish wheels, exposed upper track.
  Museum identifies 412 as Factory 112, late 1944:
  https://tankmuseum.org/tank-nuts/tank-collection/t-34-85/
  Target changed from unverified Factory 183 to the documented Factory 112.
- IS-2: museum's photograph https://parkpatriot.ru/novosti/is-2-tank-proryva/
  — six small road wheels + return rollers, large elongated cast turret,
  broad mantlet, substantial gun/brake. Restored/modernised fittings in this
  exhibit are not proof of 1944 detail; postwar stowage excluded.
- SU-100: museum's own photograph https://museedesblindes.fr/les_chars/su-100/
  — forward casemate, right commander cupola, protruding gun mount, sloped
  driver's hatch, front spare links, T-34 chassis. Postwar light cage excluded.

Images kept only in ignored data/local/visual_references/pass03. No rights to
redistribute museum photographs asserted. Original meshes only are exported.

Primary Panther-family running-gear information: US War Department, Tactical and
Technical Trends No.40, 16 Dec 1943, reproduced at
https://ww2.lonesentry.com/articles/ttt/panther-panzer-v.html : eight load-bearing
axles, front drive, .6604 m track, 3.8989 m contact-wheel span. Earlier variant;
G-specific hull detail cannot be certified from this source alone.

Acceptance still requires complete scaled SIDE/FRONT/TOP drawings, component
surveys, exact factory fittings and independent tolerance checks. This pass
must not label photo-interpreted component placement as measured historical data.
The budget permits a substantial geometry rebuild, not archival certification.
