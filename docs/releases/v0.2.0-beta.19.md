# v0.2.0-beta.19

Open Day supports a complete DualSense visitor flow. The left stick controls
analog throttle and hull steering; the right stick looks independently of the
hull and moves the gun sight; L2 zooms, R2 fires, ✕ confirms and starts a match,
○ goes back, and OPTIONS pauses or resumes. A connected controller takes
exclusive ownership of Open Day input; the mouse remains available when no
controller is connected. Player movement is manual while the MaleCNS opponent
uses a separately identified engineering layer for navigation and gunnery.

The player and MaleCNS each have six equal demo hit points. The first ten
seconds preserve the physical sight lock. Real-dataset runs use the 166,700
neuron MaleCNS backend. Recent runs confirmed continuous brain responses and
nearly real-time simulation, but the selected motor-channel signals remained
near zero; the controller's stronger movement and gunnery are therefore
engineering assistance, not a tuned biological output. The exact diagnostics
are in `artifacts/performance/open_day_five_match_validation.json` and the
separate validation outputs.

Validation completed on macOS: 28 Python tests, Godot import/combat/bridge
checks, Open Day controller and mouse regression checks, multiple real MaleCNS
matches, the arm64 packaged-backend CLI smoke test, and launch of the installed
app from a copy of the DMG. The installed GUI and physical DualSense flow still
need a manual check. Windows CI and physical Windows GUI/controller checks are
also pending. The app is ad-hoc signed and is not notarized.
