# Data dictionary

`neural_steps.jsonl` has schema `flyswarm.godot_malecns.neural_step.v1`: experiment/run/condition, seed, `neural_step_id`, simulation time, monotonic wall time, 20 ms `dt`, sensory population inputs, non-teacher observations, raw and effective six-channel traces for all 16 agents, heard audio, raw/decoded/auxiliary/adapter/final controller layers, emitted actions, tick cost, and MaleCNS metadata.

`game_steps.jsonl` has schema `flyswarm.godot_malecns.game_step.v1`: matching identifiers and neural step, simulation time, 16 agent IDs/team/vehicle, observation, position, speed, hull/turret/gun orientation, target/detection/range, alive/modules/module damage, traces/audio, control layers and applied actions, plus ticket and zone state. Rule runs have no neural-step stream; their game-step ID is the physics-step index. Their `condition` is `RULE_BASED` and they are never represented as MaleCNS responses.

`combat_events.jsonl` uses `flyswarm.combat_event.v1` and is emitted from the authoritative combat code rather than inferred from counters. Every shot has a unique `shot_id`, shooter, ammunition, launch position/velocity, target estimate, and simulation/physics timestamps. Projectile disposition is exactly one of a collision (`hit`), expiry, or still-in-flight-at-battle-end. Live vehicle hits are followed by one armor result; successful penetration, damage deltas, vehicle destruction, and projectile kill are recorded as separate events. World/terrain hits remain hits but are not vehicle hits. `validate_combat_events` checks event IDs, run identity, event order/references, totals, and nonnegative damage. The older `events.jsonl` remains a state-delta reconstruction for analysis compatibility and must not replace the authoritative stream.

`summary.json` is the existing Godot battle summary. `manifest.json` identifies one run. `protocol.json` fixes the experiment-level configuration. `validation.json` lists validity and explicit errors. `processed/run_level.csv` preserves each completed physical-side run; `processed/seed_level.csv` has one row per condition and independent seed after averaging the two side assignments. Incomplete side pairs are excluded and counted in the analysis manifest. `processed/analysis.json` contains seed-level paired effects and cautious small-sample notes. `figures/` contains PNG/SVG/PDF aggregate plots, descriptive neural/action/behavior/trajectory series, and matching numeric CSVs. Teacher labels are excluded from neural-step observation records to prevent evaluation-feature leakage.
