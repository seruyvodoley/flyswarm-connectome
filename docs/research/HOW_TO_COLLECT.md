# Collecting research data

Run `tools/run-research preflight` first. It verifies Godot, MaleCNS checksums, the real dual-brain step, validated archives, and reproducibility evidence. It prints blockers and exits nonzero until the full scientific acceptance criteria pass. Current status and tested limits are in `READY_STATUS.md`.

The controlled Godot mechanics harness is `ResearchCombatValidation.tscn`; `tools/run run-tests` executes its stationary, moving-target, moving-shooter, and mutual-fire cases. Its `controlled-combat-validation-*.json` report is a technical fixture, never scientific data. Current-source technical pilots are archived at `experiment-20261018T164727Z-72fcf668` (30 seconds), `experiment-20261018T170733Z-e42e99ed` (3-second control plumbing), and `experiment-20261018T173656Z-5a60960a` (60 seconds); exact repeats are documented in `REPRODUCIBILITY.md`. These pilots are not confirmatory. The earlier archive `experiment-20261018T154531Z-4e4d6714` predates the projectile-velocity inheritance fix and must not be cited as current-source evidence. Do not change source files while any experiment is running. The runner locks the source/config fingerprint and refuses to mix implementation versions.

`preflight` reports `platform_status` (PLATFORM_READY) and `study_status` (STUDY_READY) separately and gives category-specific blockers. Current `NON_NEURAL_TRAINED` control and a real-Godot sensorimotor tracking benchmark are not yet qualified; do not treat the Rule-AI-distilled ridge checkpoint as the trained control. A sample-size configuration is not preregistered until the primary benchmark and effect scale are defined and variance is adequate. Do not infer population effects from pilots.

Use `tools/run-research validate --run results/research/<experiment-id>` before analysis. Then run `tools/run-research analyze --run ... --reference RULE_BASED`; `tools/run-research report --run ...` prints the saved report. Collection creates a unique directory and will not overwrite one. Pilot data are operational checks and must remain labelled pilot, not final study data.
