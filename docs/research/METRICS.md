# Metrics

Primary outcome: per-run terminal ticket balance `(BLUE−RED)/600`, range −1 to 1, evaluated after both side assignments and averaged within each independent seed. The denominator is the initial total tickets (300 per team). A positive value favors the evaluated BLUE controller.

Secondary/diagnostic outcomes include terminal alive balance `(BLUE alive−RED alive)/16`, battle duration, distance, stationary/reverse time, target-visible time, target switches, shots, vehicle hits, accuracy (only when a shot denominator exists), penetrations, damage events, kills, destruction events, objective changes/capture time, mean and p99 MaleCNS tick time, Godot real-time factor, and recorded decoder/objective/gunnery/adapter interventions. The authoritative projectile log separates shot, collision, armor interaction, penetration, damage, and destruction; it validates unique shot IDs and projectile disposition. A hit or shot count alone is not a quality score. Agent rows and repeated time steps are nested in battles; the independent seed-level paired comparison is the inferential unit.

The current reporter calculates paired mean and median differences, paired standardized effect, a t-based 95% interval, seed-resampling bootstrap interval, and a sign-flip permutation p-value. Secondary p-values receive Benjamini-Hochberg q-values. It excludes incomplete side pairs, marks intervals unavailable at n=1, and labels estimates with fewer than five independent seeds as descriptive. Figures with a single observation do not draw a zero-width confidence interval. Neural-to-applied-action correlations are per-run descriptive associations only; agent/time samples are not treated as independent. Small pilot results do not establish confirmatory significance.
